About Music Player

Audiophile-grade music player with offline playback, lossless audio support (FLAC, ALAC, WAV, DSD), dynamic themes, and synced lyrics.

Key Features & Capabilities

Lossless Audio Engine

Bit-perfect playback for FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AAC, and MP3 files.

10-Band EQ & Bass Boost

Custom equalizer presets, reverb effects, and sound virtualization.

Synchronized Lyrics

Display time-synced karaoke style lyrics with offline caching.

Smart Playlists

Auto-generated playlists based on listening habits, most played, and recent additions.

Gapless Playback

Seamless transitions between audio tracks for live concerts and albums.
