Privacy Policy

DocuPulse

1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the DocuPulse mobile application ("Application", "App", "Service"), developed by Mohan MRKS ("Developer"). DocuPulse is engineered with a strict "Privacy First" and on-device execution philosophy. We believe that your personal documents, scans, signatures, and files belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Handling & Device Permissions

DocuPulse operates completely locally on your client device as an autonomous offline utility. Details regarding requested permissions:

  • CAMERA: Used exclusively to capture document pages, receipts, notes, and paper materials directly within the scanner interface. Camera preview frames and captured photos are processed strictly in memory and saved to private app storage on your device. They are never streamed or transmitted to external servers.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Granted locally on your device to import images or PDF files for scanning, recognition, and to export completed documents, multi-format archives, or signed files to locations you choose. Your files are never uploaded or synced to external cloud servers.

3. On-Device OCR & Processing Guarantee

All core computational functions in DocuPulse—including edge detection, perspective transformation, image enhancement filters, optical character recognition (OCR) via on-device machine learning models, digital signature overlays, watermarking, and multi-format document conversion (PDF, JPEG, PNG, ZIP, etc.)—execute 100% locally on your device hardware without network roundtrips.

4. Network Independence & Zero Tracking

DocuPulse contains zero advertising SDKs, zero third-party telemetry frameworks, zero tracking pixels, and zero remote analytics. The Application does not maintain user accounts, require registration, or communicate with developer-hosted cloud databases. Any outbound network interactions occur solely when you explicitly invoke system actions, such as sharing an exported document via your operating system share sheet or contacting developer support via email.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

DocuPulse does not collect, solicit, or process personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. The Application is entirely safe and appropriate for users of all ages.
