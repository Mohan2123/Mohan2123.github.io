Privacy Policy

Messages

1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Messages mobile application ("Application", "App", "Service"), developed by Mohan MRKS ("Developer"). Messages is built with a "Privacy First" approach. We believe your personal data, media, and files belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Handling & Permissions Policy

Messages operates locally on your client device as an offline utility. Permission details:

  • READ_SMS / SEND_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS: Granted locally on your device to read, compose, send, and receive SMS messages. All SMS data is stored locally in your device database and is never uploaded to any external server.
  • READ_CONTACTS: Granted locally to display contact names, avatars, and numbers for seamless conversation matching. Your contacts never leave your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Granted locally to notify you immediately of incoming text messages and message delivery status.

3. Local Message Processing & Storage Guarantee

All message databases, thread indexing, scheduled message triggers, search queries, and spam blocks occur 100% locally on client hardware via an embedded Drift/SQLite database.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry Data

The Application integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID, IP address) for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. Google AdMob is configured to comply with COPPA guidelines.
