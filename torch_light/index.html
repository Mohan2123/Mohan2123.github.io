Instant flashlight with zero delay, multi-speed strobe frequency, Morse code SOS beacon, and warm screen lighting.

Key Features & Capabilities

Instant On

Launches and turns on the flashlight with zero delay from home screen or widget.

Adjustable Strobe

Multi-speed strobe flashing for parties, visibility, and signaling.

Morse Code SOS

Automated emergency SOS flashing signal.

Colored Screen Light

Soft, glare-free reading light with custom color wheel and brightness.

Shake to Toggle

Quick shake gesture to turn on flashlight in the dark.
