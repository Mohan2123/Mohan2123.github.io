Privacy Policy

Launcher

1. Scope

This policy applies to Launcher, developed by Mohan MRKS. Launcher has no account system and does not send personal data to the developer. It has no analytics. If you use search or open a website, the query or information handled by that third-party service is governed by its own policy.

2. Information kept on your device

Launcher stores home-screen and dock layouts, folders, preferences, app names and package identifiers, and app-use counts locally so it can provide its features. This information is not uploaded to the developer. A backup contains launcher settings and layout information; it is shared or saved only through the destination you choose.

3. Optional permissions

Notification Access is used to count active notifications by app and display badges. Launcher uses the app identifier and count; it does not save or send notification text. Device-admin permission is requested only if you choose the screen-lock action. Biometric checks use your device authentication system to unlock the hidden-app area; Launcher does not receive your biometric data. You can revoke permissions in Android settings.

4. Search, websites, and third parties

When you type in launcher search, suggestions may be requested from Yandex, and a submitted web search opens Yandex. These requests include the query and are sent only when you use those features. Websites opened in the built-in browser may receive data such as your IP address and cookies under their own policies. Launcher does not control third-party services.

5. Retention and contact

Local information remains on your device until you remove it, clear Launcher data, or uninstall the app; exported backups remain wherever you save or share them. No information is knowingly collected from children by the developer. This policy may be updated when the app changes. Questions: mohanmrks@yandex.com
