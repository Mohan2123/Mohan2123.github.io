Privacy Policy

Block Blast

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Block Blast, developed by Mohan MRKS, handles information when you use the game. The game does not require an account and does not provide a developer-operated game server or cloud-sync service.

2. Information stored on your device

Block Blast stores game state and preferences locally using your device's app storage. This can include coins, unlocked levels and stars, high scores, selected block skins and backgrounds, purchased in-game items, power-up counts, sound and vibration settings, and your language selection. The game uses this information to save your progress; it is not sent to the developer by the game.

3. Device features

Audio and vibration are used locally for gameplay when enabled. The game does not use remote push notifications in its current version.

4. Sharing and support

If you choose to share game information or contact support, Block Blast opens your device's sharing or email application. Information you choose to share may be handled by that application and its provider under their own policies. Support requests can be sent to mohanmrks@yandex.com.

5. Retention and deletion

Game data remains in the app's local storage until it is removed. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Device backup or restore settings may affect whether local app data is retained by your platform.

6. Data collection

The game does not ask for personal information, operate user accounts, or include an advertising or analytics SDK in its current Flutter project. It is designed to work offline.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game changes. For privacy questions, contact mohanmrks@yandex.com.
