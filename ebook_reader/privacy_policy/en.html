1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the eBook Reader mobile application ("Application", "App", "Service"), developed by Mohan RKS ("Developer", "We", "Us"). eBook Reader is built with a "Privacy First" approach. We believe your reading data, personal book library, and document notes belong exclusively to you.

2. Storage & File Access Permissions

To provide a full document reading experience, eBook Reader operates as an offline utility. Permission details:

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Granted locally on your device to open EPUB, PDF, CBZ comics, and TXT files. We do not upload your books, bookmarks, or reading progress to any external servers.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used for audio playback controls during Text-to-Speech (TTS) reading sessions.

3. Offline & Local Processing Guarantee

Core rendering engines—including EPUB chapter parsing, PDF vector page rendering, bookmark saving, and Text-to-Speech audio queueing—are executed locally on client device hardware. No cloud document mirroring takes place.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry Data

The Application integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve banner advertisements. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID, IP address, OS version) automatically via Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving and fraud prevention.

For more detailed information, please review Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. Google AdMob is configured to comply with COPPA guidelines for ad delivery.
