Privacy Policy

QR Scanner

1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR Scanner mobile application ("Application", "App", "Service"), developed by Mohan MRKS ("Developer"). QR Scanner is built with a "Privacy First" approach. We believe your personal data, media, and files belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Handling & Permissions Policy

QR Scanner operates locally on your client device as an offline utility. Permission details:

  • CAMERA: Granted locally on your device to capture photos, scan codes, or record video directly within the app. Camera frames are processed in-memory.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Granted locally on your device to open, read, edit, and save files. Your personal files are never uploaded to any external server.

3. Local Code Processing Guarantee

QR and barcode decoding, pattern recognition, link parsing, code generation, and history storage occur 100% locally on client hardware.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry Data

The Application integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID, IP address) for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. Google AdMob is configured to comply with COPPA guidelines.
