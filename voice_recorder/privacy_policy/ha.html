2. Manufar Kula da Bayanai da Izini

Voice Recorder tana aiki a cikin na'urarku a matsayin kayan aiki ba tare da intanet ba. Cikakken bayanin izini:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Ana ba da izini a cikin na'ura don yin rikodin sauti. Ana adana sautin kai tsaye a cikin ajiyar na'urarku.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: An ba da izini a cikin na'urar ku don buɗewa, karantawa, gyarawa, da adana fayiloli. Ba a taɓa loda fayilolinku zuwa wani sabar na waje ba.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Don nuna sarrafa sauti ko matsayin aiki a bayan fage.
