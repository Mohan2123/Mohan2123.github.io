1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Voice Recorder mobile application ("Application", "App", "Service"), developed by Mohan RKS ("Developer"). Voice Recorder is built with a "Privacy First" approach. We believe your voice recordings, audio memos, and sound files belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Handling & Permissions Policy

Voice Recorder operates locally on client devices as an offline audio recording utility. Permission details:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Granted locally on your device to capture live microphone audio streams for voice recording. Microphone data is encoded directly into local storage files and is never streamed to external servers.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: Granted locally to save, manage, and play audio recording files.

3. Local Audio Encoding Guarantee

Voice recording, audio waveform calculation, MP3/AAC/WAV encoding, audio trimming, and file organization are performed 100% locally on client hardware.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry Data

The Application integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve banner advertisements. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID, IP address) for ad delivery.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. Google AdMob is configured to comply with COPPA guidelines.
