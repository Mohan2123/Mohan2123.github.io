关于 录音机

一款现代、快速且安全的录音工具。旨在帮助您轻松高效地管理音频内容。

主要功能与特性

Lossless Formats

Record in WAV (PCM), M4A (AAC), MP3, or OGG with selectable bitrates.

Noise Reduction & AGC

Automatic Gain Control and background noise suppression for crisp vocals.

Live Waveform

Interactive audio waveform display with bookmark marker support.

Built-in Audio Trimmer

Cut, crop, and save clips directly without leaving the app.

Microphone Selection

Support for external USB mics, Bluetooth headsets, and stereo recording.
