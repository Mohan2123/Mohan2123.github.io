Privacy Policy

Fruit Cutter

1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fruit Blade: Bogatyr Slicer mobile application ("Application", "App", "Game", "Service"), developed by Mohan MRKS ("Developer"). Fruit Blade: Bogatyr Slicer is built with a strict "Privacy First" and offline-first philosophy. We believe your personal data, high scores, slicing achievements, and gaming experience belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Handling & Local Storage Policy

Fruit Blade: Bogatyr Slicer operates completely locally on your client device as an offline arcade game. We do not collect, harvest, transmit, track, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). All gameplay progression—including completed levels across 100 stages, fruit encyclopedia discoveries across 50 realistic fruits, high scores, collected Rubles (₽), blade armory selections, sound pack preferences, and slicing statistics—is stored 100% locally on your device via secure client-side storage (SharedPreferences).

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Features

Fruit Blade: Bogatyr Slicer requests and uses only the absolute minimum device capabilities necessary to deliver smooth and immersive gameplay mechanics:

  • HAPTIC FEEDBACK / VIBRATION: Used purely locally to provide crisp tactile vibration during blade slices, fruit cleaving, bomb defusals, and combo chains. This feature does not access, read, or transmit any user data.
  • AUDIO & SOUND PLAYBACK: Used purely locally to output slicing sound effects, blade whooshes, juicy splashes, and celebratory victory fanfares. No microphone access, audio recording, or listening is ever performed.
  • LOCAL NOTIFICATIONS: Used strictly on-device to schedule optional daily wheel reminders and challenge notifications. The game does not utilize any remote push notification service, tracking tokens, or cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Activity

The Application does not maintain any remote user database, analytics servers, advertising trackers, or telemetry SDKs. The game functions fully without any internet connection. When you choose to initiate external actions—such as sharing your blade master high score via the system share sheet, opening the privacy policy in the built-in browser, or contacting developer support via email—you interact directly with standard system dialogs or external web endpoints that operate under their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Fruit Blade: Bogatyr Slicer contains no inappropriate content, violent gore, behavioral profiling, targeted advertising, or personal data collection. It complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy standards.
