Privacy Policy

Ludo

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Ludo Bear, developed by Mohan MRKS, handles information when you use the game. The game does not require an account and does not provide a developer-operated game server or cloud-sync service.

2. Information stored on your device

Ludo Bear stores game preferences and progress locally, including player names and colors, settings, coins, and other game state. Match history and player statistics, such as winners, turns, captures, and match duration, are stored in a local database on your device. This information is used to provide game features and is not sent to the developer by the game. Player names you choose may identify you, so avoid entering sensitive personal information.

3. Notifications and device features

If enabled and permitted by your device, Ludo Bear can schedule local reminders. These notifications are created on your device; the game does not use a remote push-notification service. Audio and haptic feedback are used locally for gameplay.

4. Sharing and support

If you choose to share game information or contact support, Ludo Bear opens your device's sharing or email application. Information you choose to share may be handled by that application and its provider under their own policies. Support requests can be sent to mohanmrks@yandex.com.

5. Retention and deletion

Game data remains in the app's local storage until it is removed. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Device backup or restore settings may affect whether local app data is retained by your platform.

6. Data collection

The game does not ask for personal information, operate user accounts, or include an advertising or analytics SDK in its current Flutter project. It is designed to work offline.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game changes. For privacy questions, contact mohanmrks@yandex.com.
