Auto Edge Detection

Automatically detects document borders and rectifies perspective distortion.

Magic Filters

Clean shadows, enhance contrast, and convert scans to crisp black-and-white.

Offline OCR

Extract searchable text from scans in 50+ languages without sending data to servers.

Multi-Page Batch

Rapidly scan entire book chapters or multi-page invoices into a single PDF.

ID & Passport Mode

Scan both sides of cards and combine onto a single standard page.
