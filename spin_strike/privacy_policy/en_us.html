Privacy Policy

Spin Strike

1. Privacy First Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Spin Strike mobile application ("Application", "App", "Service"), developed by Mohan MRKS ("Developer"). Spin Strike is built with a strict "Privacy First" and offline-first philosophy. We believe your personal data, high scores, blade armory achievements, and gaming experience belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Handling & Local Storage Policy

Spin Strike operates completely locally on your client device as an offline arcade game. We do not collect, harvest, transmit, track, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). All gameplay progression—including unlocked stages across 100 levels, accumulated in-game Rubles (₽), unlocked custom throwing blades, Cyrillic & international font styles, knife indicator symbols, and precision accuracy statistics—is stored 100% locally on your device via secure client-side storage (SharedPreferences).

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Features

Spin Strike requests and uses only the absolute minimum device capabilities necessary to deliver smooth and immersive gameplay mechanics:

  • HAPTIC FEEDBACK / VIBRATION: Used purely locally to provide crisp tactile vibration during blade impacts, wood penetrations, and knife clashes. This feature does not access, read, or transmit any user data.
  • AUDIO & SOUND PLAYBACK: Used purely locally to output throwing sound effects, satisfying fruit slices, and victory fanfares. No microphone access, audio recording, or listening is ever performed.
  • LOCAL NOTIFICATIONS: Used strictly on-device to schedule optional daily challenge reminders. The game does not utilize any remote push notification service, tracking tokens, or cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Activity

The Application does not maintain any remote user database, analytics servers, advertising trackers, or telemetry SDKs. The game functions fully without any internet connection. When you choose to initiate external actions—such as sharing your level victory status via the system share sheet, opening the privacy policy in the built-in browser, or contacting developer support via email—you interact directly with standard system dialogs or external web endpoints that operate under their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Spin Strike contains no inappropriate content, violent gore, behavioral profiling, targeted advertising, or personal data collection. It complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy standards.
