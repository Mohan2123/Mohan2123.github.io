About eBook Reader

Distraction-free reading experience with paper-like typography, customizable margins, bilingual dictionaries, and text-to-speech audio narration.

Key Features & Capabilities

Universal Format Support

Open EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, and TXT books seamlessly.

Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Listen to any book with natural-sounding offline voices.

Custom Typography

Adjust line spacing, font faces, contrast themes, and sepia warm mode.

Notes & Highlights

Highlight passages in multiple colors and export notes as Markdown.

Reading Statistics

Track reading speed, daily reading streaks, and estimated time left.
